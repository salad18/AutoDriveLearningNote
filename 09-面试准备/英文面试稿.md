> [!tip] 英文面试的 3 条铁律
> 1. **Simple sentences win**——用短句，别硬凹从句；说清 > 说漂亮。
> 2. **Structure first**——每段用 "First / Second / For example / So" 串起来，面试官能跟上。
> 3. **Slow down**——紧张会加速，刻意放慢 20%。

## 一、英文自我介绍

### 30 秒版（Elevator Pitch）

> Hi, I'm [Name], a [master's] student in [Major] at [University]. I focus on autonomous driving perception — specifically BEV perception and end-to-end driving. My most recent project is a small-scale end-to-end self-driving car, where I built the full pipeline from data collection to closed-loop testing. I'm really interested in your team's work on [方向], and I'd love to learn more today.

### 1 分钟版（Technical Round Opening）

> Hi, I'm [Name], majoring in [Major] at [University]. My focus is autonomous driving perception and planning.
>
> First, I have a solid foundation in models. I systematically studied BEV perception — LSS, BEVFormer, and PETR — as well as Transformers and end-to-end driving frameworks like UniAD and VAD.
>
> Second, I have hands-on experience. I independently built an end-to-end self-driving car project: data collection with a Raspberry Pi, training behavior-cloning models on a CPU-only machine, and closed-loop testing on a small car. I solved the distribution-shift problem — where good offline metrics don't transfer to closed-loop performance — which is exactly the challenge production teams face every day.
>
> I'm very interested in your work on [方向]. Maybe I could ask a few questions later?

### 3 分钟版（Manager Round）

> Hi, I'm [Name], majoring in [Major] at [University].
>
> **Why this field.** I started with machine learning fundamentals, and what attracted me to autonomous driving is its complexity — a single system has to handle perception, prediction, and planning together. So I set a learning path: from model foundations to BEV perception, then end-to-end driving and VLA.
>
> **What I did.** On the knowledge side, I built a personal knowledge base of 40+ teaching notes covering BEV, sensor fusion, end-to-end, and VLA, and I've read 8 frontier papers in depth. On the practical side, I built an end-to-end autonomous driving car — full pipeline from data collection to closed-loop testing — and solved distribution shift through failure-mode-driven iteration.
>
> **Why your team.** I know your team works on [方向, e.g., end-to-end deployment / world models], which aligns with my focus. In the next 3–5 years, I want to grow into an engineer who can own a perception or planning module independently.
>
> I've prepared some questions about your technical approach. I'd be happy to discuss them later.

## 二、项目深挖英文版（4 步法）

### The 4-step structure

> **Why** — the pain point. **What** — what I built. **How** — key design choices. **Result** — quantified outcome.

### 完整示例（端到端小车）

> **Why.** I wanted to validate whether end-to-end driving really works, and I only had a CPU-only machine and a small budget. So I designed a small-scale end-to-end pipeline to test the core ideas — data collection, behavior cloning, and closed-loop testing.
>
> **What.** I built a five-layer pipeline: hardware (a Raspberry Pi and a camera), data collection, three progressive models — from a single-frame CNN (DAVE-2 style), to ResNet-18 + LSTM, to a simplified BEV + Transformer — and closed-loop testing.
>
> **How.** The key challenge was distribution shift. Offline, the model only sees in-distribution data. In closed loop, its own errors feed back into the next input, so errors accumulate. I diagnosed it with failure modes — steering oscillation, hugging the edge, and weaving — and fixed each with targeted data and temporal modeling.
>
> **Result.** The track completion rate improved from [X]% to [X]% over [N] iterations. And I documented every failure mode and fix in my notes, which later became the basis of my knowledge base.

### 常见英文追问

| 问题 | 英文答法 |
|------|---------|
| Why DAVE-2 first? | Because with limited data, a simple CNN is the fastest path to a working baseline; it's also easy to diagnose. |
| What's the biggest challenge? | Distribution shift. Good offline metrics do not guarantee closed-loop performance. |
| How did you train without a GPU? | I trained on CPU and used DirectML for inference acceleration. Training is compatibility-bound; inference is performance-bound. |
| What would you improve? | Add a second camera for fusion, use a world model to synthesize long-tail data, and deploy with TensorRT on a Jetson. |

## 三、高频技术题英文答法

### Q1: Explain BEVFormer in simple terms.

> BEVFormer builds a bird's-eye-view feature map from multi-camera images. It has two key parts. First, **spatial cross-attention**: each BEV query looks at the image features it should correspond to, using deformable attention to sample a few points around its projected position. Second, **temporal self-attention**: it fuses the previous frame's BEV features, warped by ego-motion, so the model gets temporal context. The result is a unified BEV representation that downstream tasks like detection and planning can use.

### Q2: What's the difference between BEVFormer and BEVDet?

> BEVDet lifts image features to 3D with a depth distribution, then splats them into BEV. It's pure convolution-based and fast. BEVFormer uses attention — deformable attention for spatial view transformation and temporal self-attention for fusion. So BEVDet is more efficient; BEVFormer has better temporal modeling. The choice depends on the latency budget and whether temporal fusion matters.

### Q3: What is end-to-end driving?

> End-to-end driving maps sensor input directly to driving output — usually a trajectory — with a single learned model, instead of separate detection, tracking, prediction, and planning modules. The advantage is that errors don't compound across modules and the model can learn human-like behavior. The challenges are interpretability and safety validation.

### Q4: What is the role of a world model in autonomous driving?

> A world model learns to predict future scenes or states. It serves two main purposes: first, **data generation** — it synthesizes rare scenarios to feed the data loop; second, **planning** — the model can imagine outcomes of different actions and choose safer ones. DriveVLA-W0 is a good example: it uses a world model to turn unlabeled video into supervision.

### Q5: Why do VLA models matter for driving?

> VLA models add language reasoning to driving. Language gives the model a semantic prior — it can reason about "what this situation means" and produce interpretable decisions. But they also bring latency and hallucination challenges, which is why deployment usually uses a hierarchical design: VLA for high-level decisions, smaller models for low-level control.

## 四、万能句式（背下来）

| 场景 | 句式 |
|------|------|
| 开头 | "Let me start with the big picture / the key idea." |
| 分点 | "First… Second… Third…" / "There are two main reasons." |
| 举例 | "For example…" / "Let me give you a concrete case." |
| 对比 | "The difference is that…" / "Compared with X, Y is better at…" |
| 强调 | "The key point here is…" / "What really matters is…" |
| 总结 | "So to sum up…" / "In short…" |
| 没听清 | "Could you say that again, please?" / "Just to confirm, you mean…?" |
| 不会时 | "I haven't worked on that directly, but my understanding is… I'd approach it by…" |
| 反问 | "Could you tell me more about your team's approach to X?" |

## ✅ 自测一下

> [!question] Q：英文面试时语法不好怎么办？

> [!success]- 参考答案
> **语法不好不是致命问题，讲不清才是**。用短句（主谓宾）、结构词（First/Second/For example）把逻辑讲清楚，语法小错面试官会忽略。准备阶段重点练"关键术语的英文发音"（BEV、deformable attention、self-attention、end-to-end、trajectory）——术语发音错才真的扣分。
